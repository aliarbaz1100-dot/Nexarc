About NEXARC

NEXARC is a premium browser games portal made for quick, simple and enjoyable play. The platform focuses on instant access, clean discovery, mobile-friendly browsing and a smooth in-site player experience.

The library is organized across action, racing, sports, puzzle, arcade, zombie and adventure style categories so players can quickly find something to play without downloading an app or creating an account.

What NEXARC offers

  • Instant browser play on mobile and desktop.
  • Curated source-verified game catalog.
  • Search, category filters, My List, Continue Watching and share tools.
  • Fast loading UI with fallback and recovery handling.

Quality approach

NEXARC keeps catalog verification and gameplay testing separate. Source and page checks are maintained first, while final hands-on gameplay QA is completed before custom domain launch.
