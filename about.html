About NEXARC

NEXARC is a browser gaming portal focused on a smaller, heavier catalog instead of hundreds of casual ad-supported games. The active library is designed around stronger WebGL, simulation and sports experiences.

The active Heavy 20 library covers open world, racing, FPS, combat, wrestling, fighting, football, cricket, snooker, carrom, chess, cards and flight simulation.

What NEXARC offers

  • 20-game Heavy Launch Catalog.
  • No active GameMonetize or ad-supported game-provider feed.
  • Search, category filters, My List, Continue Playing and fullscreen tools.
  • Desktop + selected mobile titles, depending on each game's controls and WebGL requirements.

Quality approach

NEXARC tracks source, license, device support and performance notes per title. External open-source/public demos are loaded in the NEXARC player, while NEXARC-owned open-world, street racing, wrestling and carrom titles are already self-hosted; other curated demos can be migrated or replaced as QA requires.
